PicrossReveal - Privacy Policy

WhizCo Studio · Last updated: 3 October 2026

PicrossReveal is a puzzle game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what the game does with information. The short version: the game does not collect, send or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no internet connection. We do not receive any information about you or your device.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (solved puzzles, stars, buildings, settings) and, if you type one, a nickname are saved in a file on your own device only. This file is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

On first launch the game asks whether the player is under 13 or 13 and older. Only that answer is stored on the device, not an age or a date of birth.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game shows reminder notifications (for example, when a building is finished). They are scheduled on your device; no server is involved. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your device settings.

Children

The game is suitable for children. Because it collects no data at all, no personal information from children is collected, used or shared.

Changes

If a later version adds features that change this (for example advertising), this page will be updated before that version is released.
