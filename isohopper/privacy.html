Privacy Policy for IsoHopper: Farm Animal Hop

Developer: WhizCo Studio · Effective date: 26 September 2026

In short: IsoHopper does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases in this version. Your game progress is saved only on your own device.

1. Who we are

IsoHopper: Farm Animal Hop ("IsoHopper", "the game") is made by WhizCo Studio ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, email us at contact@whizcorps.com.

2. Information we collect

None. IsoHopper does not collect any personal information or usage data, and it does not send any information from your device to us or to anyone else. The game works fully offline.

3. Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the game keeps a small save file in its own private storage on your device. It contains:

  • Game progress: scores, coins, clovers, unlocked animals, worlds, hats, badges and similar game items.
  • Your settings, such as sound, vibration and reminders on or off.
  • An age group: "under 13" or "13 and over". The game asks for your age on first launch but stores only this group, never the age you type.
  • A nickname, only if you choose to set one in Settings. It is optional and never leaves your device. Please don't use your real full name.

This file never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone's settings.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): after your second game the game may ask to show reminders, for example when a free gift is ready. These reminders are scheduled on your device; no server is involved. You can turn them off in the game's Settings (REMINDERS) or in your phone's settings.
  • Vibration: used for short haptic effects during play. You can turn it off in Settings.

5. Photos and sharing

Photo mode saves a picture of the game to your device's Pictures/IsoHopper folder only when you press SNAP. The SHARE buttons open your phone's own share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself, and then that app's own privacy policy applies. Challenge codes contain only game information (a farm number, a world and a score), never personal information.

6. Children

IsoHopper is suitable for children and is designed to comply with the Google Play Families Policy and the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the game collects nothing, there is no personal information for us to review or delete. Parents or guardians with a question can contact us at the email above.

7. Third parties

The game contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools. It is built with the Unity engine; Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, and Unity's crash and performance reporting are turned off. Google Play, which delivers the game to your device, handles downloads and updates under Google's own privacy policy.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of IsoHopper adds anything that affects your privacy (for example advertising or an in-app purchase), we will update this page and the game's Google Play listing before that version is released, and change the effective date above.
